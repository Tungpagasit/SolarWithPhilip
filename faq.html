PG&E Electricity Costs

A PG&E electric bill reflects both the electricity a household consumes and the costs associated with generating, delivering, maintaining and operating the broader electric system.

Usage, rate plans, seasonal demand, home electrification and other factors can materially affect an individual household's bill.

The Energy Costs page provides the deeper evidence-based explanation.

Homeowners may be able to reduce electricity costs through a combination of:

  • reducing consumption
  • shifting when electricity is used
  • improving efficiency
  • storing energy
  • producing electricity at home
  • combining multiple strategies

The appropriate approach depends on the individual home.
