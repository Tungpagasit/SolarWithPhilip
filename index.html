Solar Is a Big Decision. Treat It Like One.

Going solar can be exciting, especially when the numbers and possibilities look good. But before you commit, make sure the decision is supported by more than a great presentation or an attractive monthly payment.

Why this matters

Solar involves more than the equipment. It involves you, your home, your installer, your city, your utility and the advisor helping you navigate the decisions along the way. What looks simple at the beginning becomes a journey from choosing the right solution through design, installation, approval, activation and the years that follow.

You don't have to make that decision in one conversation. Take the time to understand what you're getting, why it fits your home and what you should expect after you say yes.

Look for guidance built on knowledge and experience, delivered with integrity , and focused on helping you make the right decision for your home, not simply getting you to a signature.
