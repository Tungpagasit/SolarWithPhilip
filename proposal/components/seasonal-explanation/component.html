Seasonal battery reality

The battery can only use solar that is actually available.

For every month the model first serves daytime household use, then limits charging to remaining solar surplus, the battery’s usable energy after reserve, charge power, sunlight window and efficiency. Winter and lower-sun months therefore cannot assume a full battery every day.

Monthly production distribution is a planning approximation. Google Building Insights supplies annual property intelligence; a later Data Layers flux implementation is required before monthly seasonality can be described as Google-derived.
