NEM 3 in plain English

Grid-import and solar-export energy unit costs are not valued the same way.

Exports receive time-varying, lower-cost values rather than the full retail rate. A battery helps retain daytime solar production for night use, reducing the need to buy higher-cost utility energy when there is no solar production.

A good design must still consider timing, self-use, storage, and winter imports. It is more important to import less higher-priced utility energy than to focus on the lower credit received for exporting solar energy to the grid.
