NEM 3 in plain English

What you buy from the grid and what you export are not valued the same way.

California's Net Billing Tariff values exports using time-varying avoided-cost values rather than simply crediting every exported kWh at the full retail rate. Midday solar can therefore be exported for much less than the rate paid when the home later imports electricity. A battery helps by storing some daytime production for later use, especially after solar production falls, but storage has a daily capacity and cannot solve a seasonal shortage by itself.

This is why system design must consider when the home uses energy, when the system produces it, how much the battery can shift, and how much winter energy still comes from the utility. NEM 3 makes self-use and seasonal planning more important than a simple annual-offset percentage.
