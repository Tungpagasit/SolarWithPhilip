Understand before signing

Proposal

Upload an existing solar proposal or request a proposal based on your home, usage and goals.

Solar produces when sunlight is available. Roof direction, sunlight and shading influence production. A system may produce more during the day than the home uses at that moment, while evening and nighttime energy may come from a battery or the utility.
We compare panel count, system size, orientation and expected annual production. Size and production are not the same thing.
We review imports, exports, changing usage and why annual offset does not automatically mean zero remaining utility cost.
Exported electricity can have a different value from electricity purchased later. Timing and battery operation matter.
We review usable capacity, backup expectations, critical loads and dispatch strategy.
We identify the rate plan, escalation assumptions and remaining utility charges.
We examine scheduled payments, remaining utility costs, capital use and service obligations.
We compare who owns the equipment, who bears service and replacement responsibilities, cash requirements, contractual obligations and end-of-term choices.
We review transfer, purchase, roof removal and reinstallation provisions in the actual agreement.
What remains with the utility? What happens if usage grows? What is guaranteed? What happens after signing? We turn unknowns into a clear question list.
