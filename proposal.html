Why Philip may recommend more production

True-Up is often the part homeowners feel most.

Producing approximately the same amount of electricity a home used last year does not mean production and consumption will match each month or each hour. Winter production can be lower, evening electricity still comes from the battery or grid, and actual household use can differ from the original estimate.

A higher-production strategy creates more energy to work with across the year. Solar With Philip may also have access to dealer-level pricing flexibility that is not presented in a typical retail quote. In some situations, that flexibility may allow Philip to explore higher production without a proportional increase in the homeowner's energy cost.

The 160% scenario is Philip's higher-production planning recommendation for exploring long-term satisfaction and value. It is not a guarantee of eliminating utility charges or True-Up.
