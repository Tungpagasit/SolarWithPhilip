Why Philip may recommend more production

True-Up is often the part homeowners feel most.

Annual production equal to last year's usage does not mean production and use match by month or hour. Winter production can be lower, evening energy still comes from storage or the grid, and actual use changes.

Solar With Philip may have access to dealer-level pricing flexibility that is not presented in a typical retail quote. In some situations, Philip can explore more production without a proportional increase in the homeowner's energy cost.

The 160% scenario is a higher-production planning recommendation for exploring long-term satisfaction and value. It is not a guarantee of eliminating utility charges or True-Up.
