You want to understand or compare a solar proposal.

Welcome. A proposal comparison should reveal more than which option looks less expensive.

The most important differences are often found beneath the headline numbers: what usage was assumed, whether future electricity was considered, how production was modeled, what role the battery is expected to play, and what responsibilities come with each option.

We will begin with a shorter intake so you can move forward now. Along the way, you may notice planning questions that are easy to miss when proposals are compared only by system size, equipment, payment, or projected savings.

Come in with the proposal. Leave with better questions. You can still explore the complete Energy Strategies experience whenever you want a deeper comparison of priorities and tradeoffs.
