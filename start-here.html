We Know What Matters to Homeowners

A solar decision can affect your home, finances, utility bills and energy strategy for years. These are the questions responsible guidance should address.

Economics

  • Will I actually reduce my energy cost?
  • What will I pay over time?
  • What utility charges could remain?
  • What assumptions drive the projection?

Your Home and Roof

  • What if the roof needs repair or replacement?
  • How are penetrations and workmanship covered?
  • What happens when the home is sold?

Batteries and Reliability

  • Do I need a battery?

  • How long might backup last?
  • How does storage affect grid purchases?

Long-Term Planning

  • What if I add an EV?
