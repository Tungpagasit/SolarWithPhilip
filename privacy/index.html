Know Before You Solar.

Privacy

What happens to the information you share with Solar With Philip, described plainly.

1. Information We Collect

Get Started and Compare My Quote forms. When you use these forms you may provide:

  • First name (required), last name (optional, Compare My Quote only) and ZIP code (required).
  • How you would like to be contacted, Email or Phone, and the email address or phone number that choice requires. The other one is optional.
  • Optional context: a typical monthly electricity bill range, what you would most like to do, your electric utility, what you would most like reviewed, and a short message of up to 500 characters.
  • Optionally, a property address.
  • Optionally, an uploaded file: an electric bill, or a solar quote or proposal (a quote file is required on Compare My Quote).

Home Energy Analysis, Proposal and Build My Solar Plan tools. These tools may collect or store additional information that you enter or upload as part of their workflows, such as your name and contact details, property address, utility and bill information, a utility bill file, and the choices and results of the plan you build. To show a property view, the Home Energy Analysis sends the address you enter to Google mapping and solar services.

Technical and measurement information. See Analytics and Measurement and Browser Storage and Attribution below.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to review your request, follow up with you about it, and prepare the option, comparison or proposal you asked about. Information about your home, bill, utility or goals is used as context for that work. Listing a utility in a form does not mean Solar With Philip serves that utility, that a program is available, or that you qualify for anything.

3. How Requests Are Handled

Submitted requests are saved in the website's database and are initially handled by Philip or an authorized administrator. A request may later be assigned to an appropriate team member.

Philip or an authorized Solar With Philip representative may contact you about your request using the contact method you selected. The forms offer Email or Phone for follow-up. They do not offer text-message follow-up, and no recurring or promotional text-message program is set up through these forms.

You can also choose to text Philip yourself using the Text links on this site. That message is started by you and is separate from the form follow-up choices.

Consent line shown with the forms: I agree that Philip or an authorized Solar With Philip representative may contact me about this request using the method I selected.

4. Proposal and Service Providers

Submitting a form does not automatically send your information to outside solar companies. Your request is first received for review by Philip or an authorized administrator.

Information may later be provided to, or entered into, appropriate third-party systems, or provided to appropriate parties, when reasonably necessary to evaluate, prepare, finance or fulfill an option or proposal you requested. This can include proposal and service platforms, installers, solar or energy providers, and financing providers. Which of these are involved depends on the request. Not every request involves every kind of party.

Your information is stored with the website's database and file-storage provider, Supabase, and the site is hosted by Netlify.

5. Uploaded Documents

Uploaded proposals and electric bills are stored in a non-public storage bucket and are used in connection with reviewing, evaluating or preparing your request. Uploads are limited to PDF, PNG or JPG files of up to 10 MB. Please upload only the document you want reviewed and avoid including unrelated identification, banking or tax documents.

6. Analytics and Measurement

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and an OpenAI measurement pixel to understand how visitors use the site and how people arrive. Form events sent to analytics describe actions, such as a step being completed, and their parameters are limited so they do not include your name, email address, phone number or address. These technologies still involve information about your visit, such as pages viewed and technical details of your browser or device.

7. Browser Storage and Attribution

If you arrive from a campaign link, the site keeps the marketing parameters in the link (for example UTM tags or ad click IDs) and the page you landed on. The first visit's values are kept in your browser's local storage for up to 90 days, and the current visit's values are kept in session storage for the browser session. When you submit a form, these values are saved with your request so we can understand which page or campaign you came from. The Get Started form also keeps short-lived draft answers (such as ZIP code and goal, but not email or phone) in session storage until you submit. The Proposal and Build My Solar Plan tools also use browser storage to keep your progress while you use them.

8. Data Retention

Submitted request and customer information is stored as business records in the database. There is currently no routine automatic deletion policy. Information may still be deleted where required or appropriate.

9. Access, Correction and Deletion

To ask about the information you submitted, or to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, contact us using the details below. You can also leave optional fields blank and skip uploads.

We will review your request and respond to it.

10. California Privacy

If you are a California resident and have questions about your information, or want to ask about access, correction or deletion, contact us using the details in Section 11 below. We will review each request.
