Know Before You Solar.

Privacy

What happens to the information you share with Solar With Philip, described plainly.

DRAFT. Not approved for production. This page describes only what the current website and forms do. Items marked OWNER/LEGAL CONFIRM are not yet established and need an owner or legal decision before this page is published. Draft version draft-2026-11-v2.

Information You Provide

When you use the Get Started or Compare My Quote forms, you may provide:

  • First name (required), last name (optional, Compare My Quote only) and ZIP code (required).
  • Your preferred contact method, plus the email address and/or mobile or phone number that method requires.
  • Optional context: a typical monthly electricity bill range, what you would most like to do, your electric utility, whether you already have a quote, what you would most like reviewed, and a short message of up to 500 characters.
  • Optionally, a property address.
  • Optionally, an uploaded file: your solar quote or proposal, or an electric bill.

The Home Energy Analysis and Build My Solar Plan tools on this site collect additional information, such as a property address and utility bill details. OWNER/LEGAL CONFIRM: how those tools should be described here.

How Information Is Used

Your request is saved so that Philip or a qualified member of the Solar With Philip team can review it and follow up about the request you submitted. Information you give about your home, bill, utility or goals is used as context for that review.

Listing a utility in a form does not mean Solar With Philip serves that utility, that a program is available, or that you qualify for anything. It is context only.

Contact Preferences

You choose how you would like to be contacted: email, text, phone, or email and text. We use that choice, and we record it along with the time you submitted and the version of the consent wording shown to you.

Choosing Text means you agree to a text response about the request you submitted. It is not sign-up for recurring promotional text messages, and no automated recurring text-message marketing is set up. OWNER/LEGAL CONFIRM: final text-message consent wording and any required disclosures.

Uploaded Documents

Files you upload are limited to PDF, PNG or JPG and 10 MB. They are stored in the website's file storage and linked to your request so the team can review them. Please upload only the document you want reviewed and avoid including unrelated identification, banking or tax documents.

We do not currently scan uploaded files for malware, and we treat every upload as untrusted. OWNER CONFIRM: storage privacy settings must be verified in Supabase before any statement about private storage is added here.

Analytics and Attribution

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) and an OpenAI measurement pixel to understand how visitors use the site. Form events sent to analytics describe actions, such as a step being completed, and are designed not to include your name, email address, phone number or address.

If you arrive from a campaign link, the site keeps the marketing parameters in the link (for example, UTM tags or ad click IDs) and the page you landed on. The first visit's values are kept in your browser for up to 90 days, and the current visit's values for the browser session. When you submit a form, these values are saved with your request so we can understand which page or campaign you came from. Short-lived draft answers to the Get Started form (such as ZIP code and goal, but not email or phone) are held in your browser session and removed when you submit.

OWNER/LEGAL CONFIRM: analytics and advertising-pixel disclosures, any cookie or consent-banner requirement, and any "sale" or "sharing" characterization.

Who May Respond to Your Request

Philip or a qualified member of the Solar With Philip team may follow up about the request you submitted, using the contact method you chose.

External Sharing

The forms do not ask you to agree to share your information with installers, lenders, financing or program providers or other outside companies, and no such sharing is set up through these forms. Your request is stored with the website's database and file-storage provider, Supabase, and the site is hosted by Netlify. OWNER/LEGAL CONFIRM: whether information will ever be shared with outside companies, and the separate disclosure that would be required first.
