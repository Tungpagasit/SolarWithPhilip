Know Before You Solar.

Privacy

What happens to the information you share with Solar With Philip, described plainly.

DRAFT. Not approved for production. This page describes only what the current website and forms do. Items marked OWNER/LEGAL CONFIRM are not yet established and need an owner or legal decision before this page is published. Draft version draft-2026-11-v3.

Information You Provide

When you use the Get Started or Compare My Quote forms, you may provide:

  • First name (required), last name (optional, Compare My Quote only) and ZIP code (required).
  • Your preferred contact method, plus the email address or phone number that method requires (the other is optional).
  • Optional context: a typical monthly electricity bill range, what you would most like to do, your electric utility, whether you already have a quote, what you would most like reviewed, and a short message of up to 500 characters.
  • Optionally, a property address.
  • Optionally, an uploaded file: your solar quote or proposal, or an electric bill.

The Home Energy Analysis and Build My Solar Plan tools on this site collect additional information, such as a property address and utility bill details. OWNER/LEGAL CONFIRM: how those tools should be described here.

How Information Is Used

Your request is saved in the website's database so that Philip or an authorized administrator can review it and follow up about the request you submitted. Information you give about your home, bill, utility or goals is used as context for that review.

Listing a utility in a form does not mean Solar With Philip serves that utility, that a program is available, or that you qualify for anything. It is context only.

Contact Preferences

In the forms you choose how you would like to be contacted: email or phone. We use that choice, and we record it along with the time you submitted and the version of the consent wording shown to you.

You can also choose to text Philip directly using the Text links on this site. That text is started by you and is separate from the form follow-up choices. No recurring or promotional text-message program is set up. OWNER/LEGAL CONFIRM: whether any disclosure is needed for messages you send to Philip by text and for how those messages are handled and kept.

Uploaded Documents

Files you upload are limited to PDF, PNG or JPG and 10 MB. Uploaded proposals and electric bills are stored in a non-public storage bucket and are used in connection with reviewing or preparing your request. Please upload only the document you want reviewed and avoid including unrelated identification, banking or tax documents.

We do not currently scan uploaded files for malware, and we treat every upload as untrusted. OWNER/LEGAL CONFIRM: final upload-handling wording, including any statement about encryption or access controls, which has not been verified.

Analytics and Attribution

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) and an OpenAI measurement pixel to understand how visitors use the site. Form events sent to analytics describe actions, such as a step being completed, and are designed not to include your name, email address, phone number or address.

If you arrive from a campaign link, the site keeps the marketing parameters in the link (for example, UTM tags or ad click IDs) and the page you landed on. The first visit's values are kept in your browser for up to 90 days, and the current visit's values for the browser session. When you submit a form, these values are saved with your request so we can understand which page or campaign you came from. Short-lived draft answers to the Get Started form (such as ZIP code and goal, but not email or phone) are held in your browser session and removed when you submit.

OWNER/LEGAL CONFIRM: analytics and advertising-pixel disclosures, any cookie or consent-banner requirement, and any "sale" or "sharing" characterization.

Who May Respond to Your Request

Submitted requests are initially handled by Philip or an authorized administrator. Philip or an authorized Solar With Philip representative may contact you about your request using the contact method you selected. A request may later be assigned to another team member.

External Sharing

A form submission is not automatically sent to outside solar companies because you submitted it. It is first received for review by Philip or an authorized administrator. Information may later be used with or provided to a proposal platform, installer, solar or energy provider, financing or other service provider or system when reasonably necessary to evaluate, prepare or fulfill an option or proposal you requested. Your request is stored with the website's database and file-storage provider, Supabase, and the site is hosted by Netlify. OWNER/LEGAL CONFIRM: the final disclosure of the categories of providers and platforms involved, and how these disclosures are characterized under California law.

Data Retention

Submitted request and customer information is recorded in the database as part of business records. There is currently no routine automatic deletion policy, and information may still be deleted where required or appropriate.
