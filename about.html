Why Solar With Philip Exists

Homeowners are not looking for more pressure. They are looking for clarity.

Hi, I'm Philip.

Most homeowners are not looking for solar panels. They are trying to determine whether solar makes sense for their home, whether they should lease or own, whether batteries are worth considering, whether the proposal in front of them is a good fit, and whether they can trust the people who will carry out the work.

Too often, homeowners are asked to make a major decision before they fully understand their options or what the project journey will actually involve.

That's why I created Solar With Philip : a place to learn, ask questions, review proposals, understand tradeoffs, and make a confident decision without unnecessary pressure.

Good advice is not about finding the easiest product to sell. It is about helping the homeowner choose the right path.

Experience With Perspective

My background includes 12+ years of experience in residential and commercial solar and building energy consulting, as well as a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from California State University, Sacramento.

Your Home Deserves Care

How can you trust the installation will be done right?

This is one of the biggest concerns homeowners have, and it should be. Solar becomes part of your home, so the experience depends on more than the panels. It also depends on design, engineering, permitting, installation, inspection, and long-term support.

I may not be the person installing the equipment on your roof. My role is to help you understand who is responsible for the work, what protections and warranties are included, what questions to ask, and what to expect throughout the project.

The best projects begin with an informed homeowner, clear responsibilities, and realistic expectations.
