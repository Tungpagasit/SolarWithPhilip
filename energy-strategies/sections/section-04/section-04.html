Solar + Storage 101

Understand the system before comparing programs.

Production, storage, imports and exports all affect the result.

Daytime production

Solar serves home loads first when available. Excess may charge a battery or export to the grid.

Stored energy

A battery can move solar energy into later hours. Backup duration depends on usable capacity, load, operating strategy and available solar recharge.

California export economics

Under current net-billing rules, the value of exported energy depends on when energy is exported. Import and export timing matter.

Design target vs. system size

A 150% production/offset target means an energy-production design target. It does not automatically mean 150% more equipment or 150% more cost.
