Compare what each path delivers separately from what comes with it.

PathWhat it can deliverWhat comes with it
Stay with utilityNo solar project; continued grid service.Ongoing utility tariff exposure; no on-site production; backup requires a separate solution.
Purchase / OwnershipCustomer-owned solar/storage asset and control over the equipment, subject to the chosen financing, warranties and interconnection.Upfront cash or financing obligation; ownership responsibilities; performance and service depend on actual equipment, installer and contracts.
Lease / PPASolar/storage service without purchasing the equipment, potentially with little or no upfront capital depending on the proposal.Long-term third-party agreement, payment terms, transfer/buyout provisions and service obligations that must be reviewed in the actual contract.

Compare actual proposals. Do not assume one payment structure is universally cheaper.
