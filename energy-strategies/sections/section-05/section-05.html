Utility

Best at avoiding a project. It does not create onsite solar production, and it leaves future grid purchases tied to utility tariffs.

Purchase / Ownership

Best aligned when ownership itself matters. Economics still depend on actual system price, financing, storage needs, remaining utility costs and service responsibilities.

Lease / PPA

Best aligned when preserving capital and reducing equipment responsibility matters. Long-term payment, transfer and service terms must be acceptable.
