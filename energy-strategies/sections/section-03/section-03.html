Understand The Available Energy Sources

What can actually supply and protect a California home?

Separate the energy source from the way you pay for it.

Utility grid

Simple default source. You continue buying electricity under your utility's tariff and rules.

On-site solar

Produces electricity at the property. Storage can shift when that energy is used.

Battery storage

Stores available energy for later use and can provide selected backup capability when designed for it.

The key distinction

Once solar plus storage becomes a practical energy source, the next decision is how to acquire/use it: ownership or a third-party-owned lease/PPA. That is a financing/service decision, not a different source of sunlight.

Energy-source takeaway

The grid remains part of most home strategies. Solar plus storage can change how much energy you buy from it, when you use grid power, and how much backup capability you can design for.
