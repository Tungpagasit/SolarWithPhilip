Understand The Available Energy Sources

Why does solar become the practical choice for many homes?

Compare what is actually available at a residence before comparing solar payment programs.

Utility / Grid Electricity

Advantages

  • Already connected
  • No onsite project
  • No solar agreement

Consequences

  • Can remain a high and growing household expense
  • Rate, time-of-use, fixed-charge and tariff changes remain outside the homeowner’s control
  • EVs and other new loads increase grid purchases
  • No onsite generation or solar-storage backup

Residential Wind

Small wind systems are used, but the property needs a verified wind resource, room for a tower, setbacks, zoning approval and suitable economics.

Strength

Renewable production at specialized windy sites.

Limitation

Usually less practical for dense suburban properties and ordinary rooftops.

Ground-Source / Geothermal

Ground-source heat pumps can reduce heating and cooling energy by exchanging heat with the ground. They do not directly generate the home’s electricity.

Strength

Efficient HVAC where ground-loop installation is feasible.

Limitation

Significant site work and not a household electricity source.

Solar + Storage

Solar generates electricity close to where it is used, uses distributed roof space, has no moving parts in the rooftop array, requires no delivered fuel and produces no operational air pollution at the home. Storage can move daytime solar into later hours and can be configured for backup.

Strengths

  • Scalable to household usage
  • Suitable for many ordinary residences
  • Pairs generation with storage
  • Available through purchase or service agreements

Limitations

  • Roof, shade and electrical conditions matter
  • Requires installation and interconnection
  • Economics depend on design, usage timing and terms
Adoption and maintenance: Rooftop solar is the only one of these onsite electricity options deployed at broad residential scale in California. Exact household percentages and comparable annual maintenance averages are not shown here because the available sources do not provide one apples-to-apples measure across utility service, small wind, ground-source HVAC and rooftop solar.

Energy-source takeaway

The grid remains part of most home strategies. For ordinary California homes that want onsite electricity production, solar is the practical option to evaluate further. Adding storage can change when that solar energy is used and how backup is designed.
