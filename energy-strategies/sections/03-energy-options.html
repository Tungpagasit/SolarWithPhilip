Understand the Available Energy Sources

Why does solar become the practical choice for many homes?

Utility / Grid

Already connected, but rate changes and added-grid usage remain outside homeowner control.

Residential Wind

Requires verified wind, tower space, setbacks and suitable zoning.

Ground-Source / Geothermal

Can improve HVAC efficiency but does not directly generate household electricity.

Solar + Storage

Produces electricity at the property and can shift daytime production into evening hours.

Why this matters: Solar is the broadly adaptable onsite electricity option for many California homes.
