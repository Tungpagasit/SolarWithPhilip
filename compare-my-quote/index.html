Understanding Solar Quotes

What should I compare between solar quotes?

Compare the whole picture, not only the headline price: system size, expected production, equipment, battery, ownership structure, total cost over time, warranties and who handles service.

Two quotes can look similar but assume different electricity rates, usage or production. What matters depends on your home and utility rate plan.

Next step: send your quote above and we can help you line the assumptions up.

Why do installers estimate solar production differently?

Installers may use different shading assumptions, panel orientation, degradation rates, modeling tools and system losses.

Production estimates are projections, not guarantees. Roof condition, tilt and shade can change results.

Next step: ask each installer which assumptions its production estimate uses.

What battery assumptions should I check?

Check usable capacity, backup duration, which circuits are backed up, how the battery is expected to charge and discharge, and whether the savings estimate depends on it.

Under California's current net billing rules, how a battery is used can change its value. Your rate plan matters.

Next step: ask what is backed up during an outage and what the estimate assumes.

How do cash, financing, lease and PPA compare?

Cash and financing generally mean you own the system. A lease or PPA generally means a company owns it and you pay for its use or its power.

Ownership affects who may claim incentives, who maintains the equipment, what you owe over time and what happens if you sell your home. Terms vary by company and contract.

Next step: compare the total cost over time and the contract terms, not only the monthly payment.

What questions should I ask before signing?

  • What production and savings assumptions is this based on?
  • What is the total cost over time, including any escalators or fees?
  • Who owns the system and who services it?
  • What happens if I sell my home?
  • What permits and utility approvals are involved, and who handles them?
